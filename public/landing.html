FAQ

Common Questions

What is the Nesara QFS Lab? +
Nesara QFS Lab is a quantum financial system platform offering secure digital asset management, investment plans, and global transfers backed by distributed ledger technology.
How do I deposit funds? +
After registering, go to the Deposit section in your dashboard. Send crypto to one of our receiving addresses (XLM, XRP, USDC, or BTC), then upload your transaction proof. Our team approves within 24 hours.
When can I withdraw my funds? +
Withdrawals can be requested at any time from your dashboard. Processing takes 1–3 business days depending on the amount and network selected.
Is KYC required? +
KYC verification is required to unlock full platform features including withdrawals and investment plans. It requires a government-issued ID and takes less than 24 hours to process.
What are the investment plan returns? +
Returns range from 5% per month (Starter plan) up to 35% per month (Platinum Quantum). All plans have defined durations and payout schedules visible in your dashboard.
